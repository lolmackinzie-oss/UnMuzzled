How Unmuzzled works

Three steps. Everything else happens while you're busy enjoying the con.

  1. 1

    Make your profile

    Your fursona or con name, a photo so people can spot you on the floor, your suit status, and what you're into.

  2. 2

    Just walk around

    Start or stop with the paw button at the bottom. It works with your phone in your pocket.

  3. 3

    Accept or Pass

    When you pass someone you'd both be into, they appear under "New" on the Nearby tab. They never see a Pass. If you both accept, it's a match and you can message each other on Telegram.

Settings

Private by default

Nobody can browse a list of attendees. You only ever see people you crossed paths with.

  • A random code, never your nameBluetooth only sends a random code that changes every 10 minutes.
  • Telegram stays hiddenYour Telegram is never shown to anyone until you both accept.
  • Passing is privateThey never see a Pass. Remove someone and they won't be told.
  • No passwordsOne account per person. Your Telegram account is your login.

The short version: be 18+, be kind, ask first.

  • Don't share anyone's photos outside Unmuzzled.
  • Don't share someone's Telegram or details without their OK.
  • Hosts are responsible for their room and their guests. Follow the hotel's and the con's rules.
  • Accounts that break these rules can be hidden, suspended or banned.
